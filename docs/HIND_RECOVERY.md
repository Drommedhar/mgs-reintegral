# Hind stage recovery

`s11g` now builds its native Hind and tower sequence. All eight missing assembly blocks, 5,219 instruction words, were checked against the user's Integral Disc 1 overlay before translation. [The evidence ledger](data/s11g_recovery.json) records their extents and the overlay hash. The native actor retains its 0xA80-byte layout; camera vectors, sound position, and the cinema actor pointer now have their real C types. Radar primitives use the native ordering-table type.

`mgs_hind_helpers_test` passes 1,008 original-MIPS comparisons. There are 720 flight-state cases across timers, difficulty, angle wrap, random choices, and control modes, comparing every actor byte and ordered service traces. Original interpolation and trigonometry remain active; random values, voice queuing, and sound services are deterministic boundaries. Another 288 cases cover every group and ring position of the recovered ribbon geometry routine with no intercepted calls. The tests extract unchanged definitions from the recovered source. They do not exercise every cinematic state or the entire fight.

A Debug run used story177 and the exact pose and heading assigned by the original `sub_E596`: `(4500,220,14550)`, heading2048. The original scenario constructed Hind and executed the recovered aiming controller. Its scripted sequence returned control, ordinary controller input moved Snake 665 horizontal units across 19 movement ticks, and the run reached 1,800 VSyncs without an exception. The inspected capture shows the tower walkway and Snake. The late flight-controller breakpoint was not reached in that scenario branch. This is an isolated incoming-state fixture, not proof of campaign traversal or a completed Hind fight. See [the fixture](../tests/fixtures/s11g_story177.cdb), `build/movie-audit/hind177.log`, and `hind177.bmp`.

Scoped Aislop found zero errors or fixable warnings; eighteen nonfixable warnings concern recovered/existing function and file size. The test files have no findings. The separate rappel stage and the other Hind resource variants remain integration work.
